# Muhammed Al-Ateeqi — Personal Website
## Project Brief / Source of Truth

---

## 1. Project Overview

This is the official personal website of Muhammed Al-Ateeqi.

It is not simply a developer portfolio or an online CV.

The website is designed to function as Muhammed's digital identity:

- A professional presence for recruiters and hiring managers.
- A credibility layer for potential clients and business owners.
- A portfolio of real software engineering work.
- A personal story that explains how Muhammed thinks and builds.
- A gateway into the other sides of his identity: content creation, entrepreneurship, teaching, and business thinking.

The website should answer one simple question:

> "Who is Muhammed Al-Ateeqi, what does he build, and why should I care?"

---

# 2. Primary Purpose

The primary purpose of the website is to make it easy for anyone who encounters Muhammed to understand him quickly and accurately.

Typical entry points:

- Someone asks Muhammed: "What do you do?"
- A recruiter receives his CV.
- A hiring manager searches his name.
- A company wants to evaluate his work.
- A potential client wants to understand his capabilities.
- Someone searches for "Muhammed Al-Ateeqi" online.
- Someone discovers his social media presence and wants to know more.

The website should therefore work as:

> A professional introduction, portfolio, proof of work, and personal story — in one place.

# 9. Personal Story

Muhammed started developing an interest in computers from an early age.

His first exposure was through gaming and exploration.

At around 14, he began working as a video editor using tools such as Filmora and Sony Vegas.

During high school, he began learning programming, starting with C++.

He later created content for himself instead of only editing content for others, eventually building a significant audience on social media.

He entered Computer Science because computers had already been present throughout most of his life.

During university, he balanced:

- Education
- Freelancing
- Employment
- Programming
- Content creation
- Business
- Graduation work
- Independent learning

He later founded a laptop business called "Ateeqi Tech", selling more than 80 laptops and developing a customer-first approach based on understanding needs rather than simply selling hardware.

A major personal setback caused him to temporarily stop many of these activities.

He eventually rebuilt his momentum through university completion, leadership of his graduation project, professional software engineering, and continued technical learning.

The personal setback may be referenced as a period of rebuilding and reassessment.

Detailed romantic or private life information must NOT appear on the public website.

# 25. Motion Philosophy

Animation should support storytelling and navigation.

It should never become the content itself.

Preferred:

- Subtle transitions
- Scroll-based reveals
- Meaningful section transitions
- Micro-interactions
- Carefully controlled page transitions

Avoid:

- Constant movement
- Excessive parallax
- Animation everywhere
- Distracting cursor effects
- Long loading animations
- Animation that delays access to content

GSAP or another appropriate Angular-compatible motion solution may be evaluated later.

Technology choice should follow design requirements, not the other way around.
